## Published usage context

- HDFC describes its media kit as intended for media organisations covering the bank in an article or programme. This is not a general commercial-use licence.
- SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak and Bank of Baroda files were obtained from their public website branding. No separate reuse licence was supplied with these asset responses. They are not represented here as freely licensed assets; trademark ownership stays with the respective bank.
- Official provenance does not itself establish permission for every use. The site presents the marks solely to identify salary-account banks, with an adjacent non-partnership caption. If a bank's usage requirements cannot be met, replace its image with the bank's plain-text name.
- SVGs were checked for scripts, event handlers, foreign objects and remote resource references; all six files decoded successfully with Sharp.

The salary-profile document, enquiry form, phone/message composition and the home-plan, learning and family-occasion diagrams in `src/components/illustrations.tsx` were created for this project as lightweight inline SVGs.
